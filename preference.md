# Event Monitor Preference

Edit this file to control what lands in the Event Monitor "Recommended" column.

## Core focus

- Surface macro, policy, market structure, and liquidity regime changes first.
- Prioritize events with clear implications for crypto, AI semis, US equities, rates, USD, energy, or shipping.
- Prefer recommendations that are specific to symbols, sectors, regions, or risk buckets.

## Watch symbols and proxies

- BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP
- NVDA, AMD, SOXX
- SPY, QQQ, TLT, DXY
- XLE, CL=F

## What to suppress

- Generic PR, low-signal marketing, celebrity noise, sports, or entertainment.
- Prediction-market noise without supporting news or price confirmation.
- Repetitive headlines that do not materially change the situation.

## Recommendation style

- Recommend sparingly. The main feed already shows everything.
- The reason must explain exactly what in the event matched this file.
- Prefer fewer, higher-signal recommendations over broad coverage.
